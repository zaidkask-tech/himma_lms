# himma_lms
HIMMA (هِمَّة): Hub for Internal Management &amp; Mastery Academy. A corporate LMS tailored for HR teams to seamlessly manage mandatory compliance training and empower employees with elective skill development.

# HIMMA (هِمَّة) 🚀
**المنصة الشاملة لإدارة التدريب وتطوير الكفاءات**
*A Hub for Internal Management & Mastery Academy*

HIMMA is a lightweight, corporate Learning Management System (LMS) designed specifically for HR departments to streamline, track, and manage employee training programs. It bridges the gap between mandatory compliance training and elective professional development.

## 🎯 The Bilingual Acronym
The name **HIMMA** operates as a bilingual acronym reflecting its core structural mission:
* **EN:** **H**ub for **I**nternal **M**anagement & **M**astery **A**cademy
* **AR:** **هـ**يكلة **مـ**نظومة **مـ**هاراتنا **ا**لأساسية

## ✨ Core Features
* **Dual-Track Training System:** Clear separation between **Mandatory Compliance** (e.g., GMP, Safety, Onboarding) and **Elective Mastery** (e.g., Leadership, Advanced Software Skills).
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Distinct portals for HR Admins (to assign and track) and Employees (to learn and complete).
* **Progress Tracking & Analytics:** Real-time completion rates and compliance dashboards for the HR team.
* **Automated Notifications:** Reminders for upcoming certification expirations and overdue mandatory courses.
